- Meincke, L., Nave, G., & Terwiesch, C. (2025). ChatGPT decreases idea diversity in brainstorming. *Nature Human Behaviour*, *9*, 1114-1126. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-025-02173-x — Using ChatGPT enhanced average idea creativity but reduced diversity across participants' ideas.

## Week 5

- Prakash, A., Aggarwal, S., Varghese, J. J., & Varghese, J. J. (2025). Writing without borders: AI and cross-cultural convergence in academic writing quality. *Humanities and Social Sciences Communications*, *12*(1). https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-025-05484-6 — Argues that AI-assisted convergence in academic writing style may be a net positive internationally, levelling the playing field for non-native English speakers rather than erasing individual voice.
- Ghods, K., & Liu, P. (n.d.). *Evidence against LLM homogenization in creative writing*. https://kiaghods.com/assets/pdfs/LLMHomogenization.pdf — Unpublished class project arguing that apparent LLM stylistic homogenisation largely disappears when models are given even a small amount of context; the authors find LLMs can complete a creative trajectory with as much diversity as human authors. A direct challenge to the received wisdom.

### AI Agents and Academia
- **URL:** https://gvrkiran.substack.com/p/ai-agents-and-academia
- **Author:** Kiran Garimella
- **Key points:**
  - Sharp distinction between chat interfaces and agents (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI) that operate with full project context, read/modify files, iterate autonomously
  - Agents clear the "not obviously bad" bar easily but don't elevate work to excellent - the 80% becomes cheap, the top 10% stays hard
  - *Nature* study of 41M papers: AI-assisted research clusters around popular topics, covers 4.6% less intellectual territory than conventional studies
  - *Science* study: LLM users publish 30-90% more papers, but gains concentrate among already-trained researchers
  - "Grunt work was training" paradox: data cleaning and basic coding used to build researcher skill, now done in minutes by agents. Value shifts to questions asked and data uniquely accessed
  - No individual incentive to slow down even when collective outcomes deteriorate
  - Author chose not to admit PhD students because traditional apprenticeship learning disappears when execution is cheap
- **Relevance to course:** Validates chat -> grounded -> agents progression. Strengthens convergence/homogenization theme with empirical evidence. Frames the quality ceiling problem that agent weeks need to address.
